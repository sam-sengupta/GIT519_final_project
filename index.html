FUEGUIA 1833

Shop all perfumes

Fueguia is a pioneering perfume house founded in 2010 by artist Julian Bedel in Buenos Aires, Argentina, with its headquarters in Milan, Italy.

Embracing the concept of ethnobotanical perfumery, Fueguia combines scientific research with artistic exploration to create evocative, sustainable, and deeply personal scents. Committed to artisanal craftsmanship and environmental preservation, Fueguia continually pushes the boundaries of fragrance innovation while honoring the delicate balance of nature.

Inspired by the boundless virgin territory of South America, an inexhaustible source of stimuli, Fueguia 1833 was conceived as an homage to the history, art, music, nature, and local communities that distinguish the vibrant culture of the territory, represented through every collections: Alquimia, Antropología, Armonias, El Mundo de Ouds, Destinos, Fábula Fauna, Linnaeus, Literatura, Muskara and Personajes.

JULIAN BEDEL
